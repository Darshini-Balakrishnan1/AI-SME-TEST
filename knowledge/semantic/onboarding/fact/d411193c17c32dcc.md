The onboarding page is a practical first-week checklist. It recommends working top to bottom on day one: confirm access to required tools, review the team space and key documentation, meet with the manager to clarify first-week expectations, identify primary communication channels, and note support routes for people/payroll/technical issues. It then serves as a first-week progress tracker and advises asking the manager or relevant support team early when unclear.
